# Code of conduct

**English** | [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be kind, stay on topic, and assume good intent.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/octrow). Reports are kept confidential.
